Full guide — how the scoring works

SPS (Squeeze Potential Score) is a 0–100 overall score made from four pillars:

PillarWeightMeaning
Structural35%Short interest, days to cover, borrow cost, utilization, float size
Velocity30%Pre-market price change, relative volume, front-week call activity (gamma)
Catalyst20%Upcoming earnings or a large pre-market gap
Narrative15%Meme / retail story strength + current attention

Readiness colors

  • 🟢 GREEN = SPS ≥ 70 and Structural ≥ 55 and Velocity ≥ 48 → strongest setups
  • 🟡 YELLOW = SPS ≥ 54 and at least two solid pillars → watch list
  • ⚫ BLACK = missing key pieces right now

Best time to use this
7:30 AM – 9:30 AM ET (pre-market), when velocity and gamma data are most meaningful.
