How this league bends value

  • All the round 1–2 studs come back. Chase, Gibbs, Bijan, CMC, Nacua, Barkley, Jeanty, CeeDee, AJB, Josh Allen, Lamar, Hurts and more were 2025 R1–2 picks — ineligible to keep, so the top of this draft is loaded and your early picks matter enormously.
  • Rushing QBs are the cheat code: pass yds pay 1/25 with 4-pt TDs, but QB rushing pays full RB rates (0.1/yd, 6-pt TD). Josh Allen, Daniels, Hurts, Dart outperform their pocket-passer peers. Pocket QBs (Burrow, Goff, Stafford) are fine but fungible — never reach.
  • 8 RB/WR/TE starters per team (96 league-wide) — the flex depth dries up around round 10. Bank starters early; take upside swings after R12.
  • Non-PPR: fade target-hog slot WRs and pass-catching backs; favor TD equity and yardage (big-play WRs, goal-line RBs).
  • No kicker or DST — all 18 picks are skill players. Late rounds = handcuffs and rookie lottery tickets (Monangai, Coleman, Tyson, Lemon, Concepcion).
  • Watch injury news before draft night: Mahomes, Nabers, Skattebo, and Jayden Daniels all carried July question marks.
